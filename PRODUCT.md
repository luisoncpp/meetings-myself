# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A solo personal planner using a local-first desktop app for daily execution and weekly reflection. Their context is private, offline-capable planning synced across devices via Google Drive — not a team workspace or shared productivity suite. The job to be done: decide what matters today, work through it with calm focus, maintain disciplined structure over time, and gain reflective clarity at week boundaries without gamification or dashboard noise.

## Product Purpose

A local-first personal planning app built around Daily Plans, Weekly Reviews, and a standing Library of Values, Goals, Habits, and Tasks. It exists to make deliberate daily action feel calm and structured, while weekly reflection produces honest clarity — not streak anxiety or KPI theater.

Success looks like: opening the app lands on a trustworthy Daily Plan home; choosing and ordering today's work feels deliberate and uncluttered; habits and tasks reflect real outcomes without punishment loops; the Library and Weekly Review are always available but never compete with the daily center of gravity.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, disciplined, clear.

Voice is calm and confident — supportive structure without hustle culture, corporate chrome, or playful gamification. Emotional goals: calm focus during daily execution, disciplined structure that does not feel rigid, and reflective clarity when closing a Calendar Week.

## Anti-references

- **SaaS productivity dashboards** — dense widget grids, hero metrics, KPI cards, purple-gradient accents, and "workspace OS" density that treats planning as analytics.
- **Gamified habit apps** — streaks, badges, confetti, leaderboard energy, and streak-anxiety loops that replace reflection with performance pressure.

## Design Principles

1. **Daily Plan is home** — The first screen earns trust and attention; Library and Weekly Review are peers in capability but not in prominence.
2. **Calm over clever** — Reduce cognitive load; no dashboard theatrics, no gamified nudges, no visual competition between today's work and everything else.
3. **Structure without rigidity** — Ordering, cadence, and lifecycle rules feel supportive; forward-only propagation is honest without feeling punitive.
4. **Reflect, don't score** — Weekly clarity comes from preserved reflection and correctable outcomes, not points, streaks, or anxiety loops.
5. **Show the real state** — Archived, overdue, and corrected entries stay visible in context; the UI mirrors domain truth instead of hiding messy history.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline for v1: sufficient color contrast (including placeholders and muted text), full keyboard operability for core flows, visible focus states, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for any motion beyond essential feedback.
